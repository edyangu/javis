Privacy Policy

Javis collects only the information needed to issue and validate your concert ticket: your name, phone number, ticket type, and payment method. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Payment information is processed by MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money through their secure channels. Javis does not store your mobile money PIN or full payment credentials.

Ticket codes are generated per purchase and are tied to your phone number. If you lose a code, contact the organiser with the phone number used for purchase.

Data is retained for the duration of the event and a reasonable period afterwards for dispute resolution, after which it may be deleted.

For questions, contact the organiser listed on your ticket.

Terms of Service

1. Tickets are non-transferable unless the organiser permits it. Each code grants one entry.

2. Ticket prices are set by the organiser and may change before purchase. The price shown at the time of payment is the price you pay.

3. Entry is subject to the event's entry time and location. Late arrivals may not be admitted.

4. Refunds are handled by the organiser according to their own policy. The demo system does not process refunds automatically.

5. Fraudulent codes, duplicated codes, or codes that fail verification will be denied entry.

6. The organiser reserves the right to refuse entry to anyone for safety or misconduct reasons.

7. This demo system stores data on Firebase servers. In production, additional security measures such as App Check and rate limiting will be applied.

Cookies and Local Storage

When you first visit Javis, a consent dialog asks you to agree to the privacy policy and terms. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage.

We do not use tracking cookies. We do not use analytics scripts. We do not use advertising cookies. Local storage is used only to remember your consent and minor interface preferences.

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to reset your consent choice. Doing so will make the consent dialog appear again on your next visit.
